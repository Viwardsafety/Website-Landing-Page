Our Methodology

How We Work

A structured, evidence-based approach that delivers rigorous safety outcomes with full auditability at every stage.

01
Understand & Scope

We begin by thoroughly understanding your system, operational context, and safety obligations to define a focused, proportionate engagement.

02
Identify & Assess

Using recognised methodologies — HAZID, FMEA, FTA, bowtie analysis — we systematically identify hazards and assess their risk significance.

03
Manage & Substantiate

We develop practical mitigation strategies and provide the engineering substantiation to demonstrate that risks are reduced to As Low As Reasonably Practicable (ALARP).

04
Report & Assure

Clear, defensible documentation — Safety Case Reports, audit findings, and periodic reviews — that stand up to regulatory and independent scrutiny.
